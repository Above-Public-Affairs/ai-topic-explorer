# 7.2 iPhone Safari test: Safari Technology Preview at phone size

**Tested:** 2026-11-11, live site https://aitopicexplorer.app (main at b312fd3)
**Tester:** Claude (task 7.2 session)

## What this is, and what it isn't

**This is desktop WebKit (Safari Technology Preview on macOS) sized to a phone. It is not iOS Safari and not the iOS Simulator.** The Simulator was the plan, but this Mac has only the Xcode command-line tools and no full Xcode, so Josh chose an STP pass instead.

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Browser | Safari Technology Preview, WebKit 605.1.15, "Version/27.0" |
| User agent | Desktop macOS Safari. The STP tools can't override the UA, so no iPhone UA was sent. |
| Device pixel ratio | 1 (an iPhone is 3) |
| Viewports | **390×852** portrait, standing in for iPhone 15 (390×844); the window couldn't go 8px shorter on this screen. Then **375×667** (small phone) and **844×390** (landscape). |
| How sized | The `set_viewport_size` tool didn't work (it stayed at 633×493), so I resized the STP window with AppleScript and checked `innerWidth`/`innerHeight` each time. Afterwards I put the window back to its original size. |

**Screenshots:** the tool's viewport screenshots came out blank on most pages, so every screenshot here is a crop of a full-page capture. Full-page capture doesn't render `backdrop-filter` blur or fixed-position backgrounds correctly. Translucent headers and dialogs therefore look more see-through in these images than they do in the browser, and the page background sometimes changes tone partway down. Read those as capture artifacts, not bugs.

**Data and cost:** I reused the two existing results pages (`cmwcng8dz00lh0xppa2biv1tx`, `cmwcn6ywq00g40xpp50l3kq7i`) and ran **one** fresh analysis for the in-progress screens: "Rooftop beekeeping rules in small coastal towns", now at `/results/cmwcpczaj00q20xppsykjdy2m`. The bug report form was not submitted.

**GA:** I set `window['ga-disable-G-6WT4RP9NQD']=true` after each page load, because there was no way to inject it earlier. Each navigation's page_view (about 12 of them) probably reached GA. Later events in each page, including the share and analysis events, were blocked.

## Issues

Severity scale: blocker / major / minor / polish.

### 1. "Show less" leaves the reader thousands of pixels down the page (major)

This is the same issue 7.3 found in Chrome; it also happens in WebKit.

- **Steps:** at 390 wide, open `/results/cmwcn6ywq00g40xpp50l3kq7i`, Answers, Claude tab. Tap "Read the full answer" (page grows from 8,696px to 11,484px), scroll to the bottom of the answer, tap "Show less".
- **Result:** scrollY stays at 4,310 while the page shrinks back. The collapsed answer card now ends 1,252px *above* the viewport, and its "Read the full answer" button is 2,318px above. The reader lands at the end of Arguments and the start of Named Entities, with no sign of where they were.
- **Expected:** after collapsing, scroll so the answer card (or its tab bar) is back in view.
- **Screenshot:** `screenshots/7.2/05-show-less-landing.png`

### 2. Dialogs closed by click or tap don't return focus to the button that opened them (minor)

This confirms 7.1's finding. It matters on iPhone because iOS Safari also doesn't focus a button when it's tapped.

- **Steps:** open "How does this work?", a theme ("Evidence limitations"), the Changelog or "Report a bug" with a click, then close it with its × or Esc.
- **Result:** focus goes to `<main>` for the How it works and theme dialogs, and to `<body>` for Changelog and Report a bug. For the How it works modal the page also scrolled 30px on close, apparently from focusing `<main>`. If the button is focused first and the dialog is opened with Enter and closed with Esc, focus returns correctly. So the dialogs restore whatever had focus when they opened, and after a click or tap in WebKit that's nothing.
- **Expected:** return focus to the button that opened the dialog, whether it was opened by keyboard, click or tap.
- **Screenshot:** `screenshots/7.2/02-how-it-works-modal.png`, `screenshots/7.2/06-theme-dialog.png` (states, not the focus itself)

### 3. Bug report textarea is 14px, so iOS Safari will zoom the page when it's focused (minor, inferred)

- **Steps:** open "Report a bug" and tap the description box.
- **Result:** the computed `font-size` is 14px (`text-sm`). iOS Safari zooms into any input under 16px when it gets focus, and the page stays zoomed after the dialog closes. The topic input is 16px and won't zoom. I inferred this from that iOS rule; STP can't show the zoom.
- **Expected:** 16px on the textarea at phone widths.
- **Screenshot:** `screenshots/7.2/08-bug-report-form.png`

### 4. Share copies the link but never opens the iOS share sheet (minor)

- **Steps:** on a results page, tap Share.
- **Result:** `handleShare` (`components/ResultsShell.tsx`) only calls `navigator.clipboard.writeText` and shows "Copied". On iPhone people expect the share sheet (Messages, Mail, AirDrop), which `navigator.share` would open where it's supported, with copy as the fallback.
- **Expected:** a product call for 7.4. The copy itself works.
- **Screenshot:** `screenshots/7.2/03-results-top-390.png` (Share button)

### 5. Shared results links have no preview image (minor)

- **Steps:** `curl` a results URL and look for `og:image` or `twitter:image`.
- **Result:** the home page has `og:image` (`/opengraph-image`), but results pages have none. They still declare `twitter:card=summary_large_image`, and their `twitter:title` is the generic "AI Topic Explorer" while `og:title` names the topic. A link shared in iMessage, Slack or X shows no image.
- **Expected:** give results pages the site OG image, or one per topic, and use the topic in `twitter:title`.
- **Screenshot:** none (found in the page HTML)

### 6. The sticky results header takes a third of a landscape screen (minor)

- **Steps:** open a results page at 844×390 and scroll down.
- **Result:** the mobile header (topic, Share/New Topic, AI status row, sections nav) is 129px tall and always visible: 33% of a 390px landscape viewport, 15% at 852 portrait and 19% at 667. In landscape about 260px is left for reading.
- **Expected:** a shorter header in landscape or on short screens, for example hiding the AI status row or collapsing the header on scroll.
- **Screenshot:** `screenshots/7.2/12-landscape-sticky-header.png`

### 7. "Click any theme to see it in context" on touch screens (polish)

- **Steps:** results page, under the Key Themes cloud.
- **Result:** the hint says "Click" on a phone. "Tap" or a neutral "Select" fits touch better.
- **Screenshot:** `screenshots/7.2/06-theme-dialog.png` (behind the dialog)

### 8. Some tap targets are under 44pt (polish)

- **Steps:** home page at 390.
- **Result:** the example topic chips are 38px tall and "How does this work?" is 36px. That meets WCAG 2.2 AA (24px) but is under Apple's 44pt guideline. Everything else measured 40–52px.
- **Screenshot:** `screenshots/7.2/01-home-390.png`

### 9. While analyzing, the topic box shows the end of the topic and hides the start (polish)

- **Steps:** submit "Rooftop beekeeping rules in small coastal towns" at 390 wide.
- **Result:** the read-only input stays scrolled to the caret, so it shows "beekeeping rules in small coastal towns" with "Rooftop" cut off.
- **Expected:** scroll the input back to the start when it locks (`scrollLeft = 0`).
- **Screenshot:** `screenshots/7.2/13-analyzing.png`

## Not covered

What only a real iPhone check would add:

- **On-screen keyboard and focus zoom.** Whether the page jumps when the keyboard opens, whether Explore stays visible above it (at 375×667 it sits at y=486, which a keyboard may cover), and whether the 14px bug textarea really zooms (issue 3).
- **Safe areas.** Notch or Dynamic Island, the home indicator, and the floating bug button and sticky header near the edges, in both orientations.
- **iOS share sheet and clipboard.** Real iOS clipboard permission behaviour and the missing share sheet (issue 4).
- **Real touch.** Tap feel, `active:scale` press feedback, scroll momentum, rubber-banding inside the changelog dialog, accidental taps while scrolling theme pills, swipe-back.
- **Performance.** A 3x screen, mobile CPU and GPU (heavy `backdrop-filter` blur on the sticky header and dialogs), memory on the ~11,000px expanded results page.
- **Real network.** Cellular latency and loading states on a slow connection.
- **Mobile Safari itself.** The iOS user agent, the collapsing address bar and how `100vh` and `min-h-screen` behave with it, dark mode, Reader mode, text-size settings, VoiceOver.
- **Visual check of blur.** This capture tool can't render blur, so frosted dialogs and the header weren't checked by eye.
